# Day-0 Prohibitions

These are absolute development prohibitions.

1. Do not change architectural ownership without explicit approval.
2. Do not change security authority without explicit approval.
3. Do not change trust boundaries without explicit approval.
4. Do not change deployment topology without explicit approval.
5. Do not change fundamental model architecture without explicit approval.
6. Do not guess when architecture is unclear.
7. Do not perform destructive or irreversible operations without required approval and controls.
8. Do not violate Constitution/Day-0 prohibitions to complete a task.

This is the authoritative home of the Day-0 list. Other documents must not redefine or expand it.
