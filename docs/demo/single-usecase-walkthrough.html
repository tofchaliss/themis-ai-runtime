Remediating CVE-2022-32149

A real Finding in Themis is remediated by a governed AI execution. Themis stays the system of record from the first byte to the last: it mints the commission, the harness runs inside it, the intake reconstructs the run from the record alone, and two different humans propose and decide. Every value on this page is copied from the record; none is illustrative.

Operator · themis-intake

Themis reconstructs the execution from the record alone

The intake receives only the tuple (anchor hash, task id, artifact-bound seq). It re-derives everything else: the commission from the CREATED origin, the five production links, the verification re-run over the egressed bytes, the business references from the Finding bytes the model actually read. The model's output is never an input.

What this proves, and what it does not

Established

  • Themis minted the authority (commission) before any execution and derived it back from the record, never from the caller.
  • The harness read the Finding as a governed record over the read door and never wrote security truth.
  • The execution reconstructs from the record alone, five links, verification re-run over egressed bytes, on two machines.
  • Proposal and decision are two humans with two keys; the model authored an artifact, not a decision.
  • The same walk failed twice under rsys@6 and the record showed why; the amendment was grilled, locked and gated before rsys@7 existed.

Not established

  • That the remediation is correct. The evidence class stays inferred; the decider owns that judgement.
  • Model quality. The gate proves gpt-oss:20b completes this skill; qwen2.5:7b does not, recorded as a capability observation.
  • Anything beyond one host: the two-VM topology, subject-bound scope (D-I-9) and product-scope write confinement have their own grills.
